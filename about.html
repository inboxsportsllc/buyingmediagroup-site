About

Buying Media Group is the parent company of Buying Sandlot and Buying Sideline. We build expertise and relationships in one market before we expand to the next.

What we cover

Buying Sandlot covers the business of youth sports: clubs, leagues, facilities, training, events, software and finance.

Buying Sideline covers sports hospitality and premium experiences: venues, access, food and beverage, technology and sports travel.

Who we serve

Operators, service providers and investors in youth sports. Team and venue revenue leaders, rights holders, college athletic departments, hospitality sellers and sports travel specialists in premium experiences.

How we work

People report, produce and edit our journalism. Readers come first. Advertising does not buy coverage or favorable treatment, and paid content is always clearly labeled.
